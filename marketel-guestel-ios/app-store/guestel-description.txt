Guestel keeps the hotels you book directly in one place.

Save a property once and it stays in your Guestel wallet — its rooms, your reservations, and a direct line to the front desk, without going through a booking site.

• Save participating hotels, inns and motels to your wallet
• Browse rooms and book directly with the property
• Hold a room with a temporary $1 card authorization, then pay the property directly
• Keep reservations and upcoming stays organized
• Message the front desk about your stay
• Get booking, message and check-in updates
• Come back and book the same property again

Guestel is for guests of participating properties. It is not a hotel marketplace and does not aggregate or resell third-party inventory — a property appears in Guestel only when you open its own booking link or App Clip.
