Marketel support

Help with Inspect reports, signing in, notifications, bookings, availability, your direct booking page, or your Marketel account.

Email support

Signing in

Inspect uses a six-digit email code. Front Desk uses the email connected to an active Marketel property; you can also sign in with its property ID and Front Desk PIN.

Inspect reports

An unfinished report is saved on the device while you work. Verify your email to save it online, finalize it, export a PDF, or create a private share link. A finalized version cannot be edited; create a new report to make corrections.

Booking alerts

After signing in, allow notifications so Front Desk can alert you to bookings and actions that need attention. If alerts are off, open the menu in the top-right of the app, choose Notification settings, and enable notifications in iOS Settings.

Account or data deletion

An Inspect operator can open Account and permanently delete the separate Inspect account and reports. A property owner can open Your Page, scroll to Privacy & account, and request booking-property deletion; that request has a 7-day grace period. You can also contact support for help.

Response time

Email support@bookmarketel.com. Include your property name and the email on the account. We aim to respond within two business days.
