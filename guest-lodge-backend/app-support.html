Front Desk support

Help with signing in, notifications, bookings, availability, your direct booking page, or your Marketel account.

Email support

Signing in

Use the email connected to your active Marketel property and enter the six-digit code we send. You can also sign in with your property ID and Front Desk PIN.

Booking alerts

After signing in, allow notifications so Front Desk can alert you to bookings and actions that need attention. If alerts are off, open the menu in the top-right of the app, choose Notification settings, and enable notifications in iOS Settings.

Account or data deletion

The property owner can open Your Page, scroll to Privacy & account, and request account deletion. A deletion request can be canceled during its 7-day grace period. You can also contact support for help.

Response time

Email support@bookmarketel.com. Include your property name and the email on the account. We aim to respond within two business days.
