Questions

Which PMS does it work with?

Most PMSs your team opens in a web browser. Cloudbeds and BookingCenter connect directly, and we’ve tested the agent on Little Hotelier. Using something else? Start the trial and tell us which one. If we can’t connect it, you don’t pay.

Do I have to change anything I use now?

No. Keep your PMS, your channel manager and your website. Your booking page gets its own link; put it behind the Book Now button on your site.

Is giving you a PMS login safe?

Make a separate staff user just for Marketel, so you can switch it off at any time without touching anyone else’s login. The agent only enters and checks bookings, and we keep a screenshot of every step it takes.

What if a booking goes in wrong?

It isn’t saved until a second AI has checked the form against the guest’s booking, and the agent reads it back from your PMS afterwards. If anything doesn’t match, it stops and a person on our team sorts it out.

What does it cost?
