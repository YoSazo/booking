Which PMS does it work with?

Any PMS your team logs into in a web browser. Cloudbeds and BookingCenter also connect directly. If we can’t connect yours, you don’t pay.

Is giving you a PMS login safe?

Make a separate staff user just for our agent, so you can switch it off any time without touching anyone else’s login. It only checks rooms and enters bookings, and we keep a screenshot of every step it takes.

No. Keep your PMS, your channel manager and your website. Put your new booking site’s link behind the Book Now button on your website.
