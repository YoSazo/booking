Questions

What do I get for $97?

We build your booking page with your rooms, photos and rates, connect it to your PMS, test a booking end to end, and send you the link, all within 24 hours. The $97 covers the setup and your first month. After that it’s $49 a month.

Can I pay once instead of monthly?

Yes. Pay $497 once and there’s no monthly fee, ever. Choose “Pay once” in the plans above. Same engine, same 24 hours, same 30-day money-back guarantee.

Which PMS does it work with?

Cloudbeds and BookingCenter connect directly. Little Hotelier goes through our agent, which entered 50 of 50 test bookings correctly. Most other PMSs your team opens in a web browser work the same way. If we can’t connect yours, you get your money back.

What do you need from me?

Two things, after you order: a separate staff login for Marketel in your PMS, and your website or a few photos of your rooms. We text or email you for both.

What if the agent can’t enter a booking?

A person on our team enters it by hand within a minute. Every booking reaches your PMS either way. The agent has already entered 250+ bookings into hotel PMSs.

Is giving you a PMS login safe?

Make a separate staff user just for Marketel, so you can switch it off at any time without touching anyone else’s login. The agent only enters and checks bookings, and we keep a screenshot of every step it takes.

Do I have to change anything I use now?

No. Keep your PMS, your channel manager and your website. Your booking page gets its own link; put it behind the Book Now button on your site.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Reply to any email from us or write to support@bookmarketel.com. And for 30 days you can ask for your money back, no questions asked.
