  • You tried paying a little extra when you could.

But those were never 3 separate problems. They were 3 symptoms of the same thing:

Your minimum payment shrinks every month, and your progress shrinks with it.

On minimums:

  • Your Payment

    Every month the bank asks for a little less, so less goes to what you owe.

  • Your Interest

    On a high-rate card, most of each minimum goes to interest, not the balance.

  • Your Date

    Your debt-free date slides decades away: in our example, January 2052.*

    You pay every single month and the balances barely move.

Is this just the snowball or avalanche method?

No, and that's exactly the point. Payoff runs five plans side by side on your actual numbers: smallest balance first (snowball), highest rate first (avalanche), cash-flow first, biggest interest cost first, and minimums only, so you can see what you're up against. Then it shows you which one gets you out soonest and how much interest each one costs.

Is this a spreadsheet? Do I need Excel?

Nope! Payoff is an app that opens right in your phone's browser. Nothing to download, no Excel, no Google Sheets, no formulas to break. It works on your phone, tablet or computer.

How hard is it to use?

You enter 3 numbers for each debt: the balance, the interest rate and the minimum payment. They're all on your monthly statement. Payoff does all of the math the moment you add them.

Does it connect to my bank? Is my information safe?

Payoff never asks for a bank login. You type in your balances yourself, and they're saved on your own phone, not on our servers.

Is this a one-time fee or are there recurring charges?

One time. You pay once and Payoff is yours. No subscription, no renewals, no surprise charges. The Cash-Flow Calendar and the Spending Reset are optional and one-time too. After you pay, you can add the optional Payoff Coach ($9 a month or $67 a year, cancel any time), but only if you choose it.

When do I get access?

Right away! The moment you pay, Payoff opens on your phone, and we email you a link so you can come back to it any time.

Fair question, and we'd ask it too. Payoff is software: we built it once, so we can sell it to a lot of people for very little. It's a one-time payment, there's no subscription, and it's backed by our 30-day money-back guarantee.
