A few things you might be wondering

Is this a spreadsheet?

No. The planned product is a phone-friendly web app: enter your numbers, compare the plans and follow a schedule. No spreadsheet formulas to edit.

What information do I need?

Your current balances, annual interest rates and monthly minimums, plus the total you can pay each month. Check your latest statements for those figures.

Will it always find a faster way?

No. The result depends on your inputs and the model assumptions. Some approaches produce the same finish month. Payoff should show the comparison, not promise a particular saving.

Do I have to pay more every month?

You choose the budget. Plans can be compared using the same amount. When one debt finishes, its payment can go toward the remaining debts instead of reducing the total you pay.

Is this a subscription?

The proposed base offer is $33 once. No automatic renewal. Optional extra tools have separate one-time prices and start unchecked.

Does it connect to my bank?

No bank connection is planned. You enter and update your numbers yourself. Your lender’s statement remains the source of truth.

Where will my financial data be saved?

The planned app saves on your device, with an export for backups. Restoring a purchase restores access, not the financial data from another phone. No financial data is collected by this preorder page.

Can it explain the plan to me?

Clear explanations are part of the planned product. An AI assistant is being considered, but unlimited AI use is not included or promised with this preorder.
