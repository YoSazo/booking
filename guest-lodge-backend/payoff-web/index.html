A few things you might be wondering

Why pay for this instead of using a free calculator?

Payoff keeps your debts, five strategy comparisons, what-if calculations and monthly payment schedule together. You can update your balances and save your plan as a PDF without rebuilding a spreadsheet.

Can I use it on my computer too?

Yes. Open your emailed access link on your phone, tablet or computer. Each device saves its own debt numbers locally.

What if I have a lot of debts?

You can enter up to 50 credit cards and loans, then see how the plans compare using the monthly budget you choose.

Is this a spreadsheet?

No. Payoff is a phone-friendly web app: enter your numbers, compare the plans and follow a schedule. No spreadsheet formulas to edit.

What information do I need?

Your current balances, annual interest rates and monthly minimums, plus the total you can pay each month. Check your latest statements for those figures.

Will it always find a faster way?

No. The result depends on your inputs and the model assumptions. Some approaches produce the same finish month. Payoff should show the comparison, not promise a particular saving.

Do I have to pay more every month?

You choose the budget. Plans can be compared using the same amount. When one debt finishes, its payment can go toward the remaining debts instead of reducing the total you pay.

Is this a subscription?

No. Payoff is $33 once. No automatic renewal.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You enter and update your numbers yourself. Your lender’s statement remains the source of truth.

Where will my financial data be saved?

On your phone only. Your balances are never sent to us. Your emailed link opens Payoff on any device, but each device keeps its own numbers.

What about refunds?

30-day money-back guarantee. Email support@bookmarketel.com with your order reference within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, including any extras you bought.

When do I get it?

Right away. After you pay, Payoff opens on your phone, and we email you the link so you can come back to it.
