Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just the snowball or avalanche method?

No, and that’s exactly the point. Payoff runs five plans side by side on your actual numbers: smallest balance first (snowball), highest rate first (avalanche), cash-flow first, biggest interest cost first, and minimums only, so you can see what you’re up against.

Then it shows you which one gets you out soonest and how much interest each one costs. Sometimes it’s snowball. Sometimes it’s avalanche. Sometimes it’s one you’ve never heard of… and you’ll know in about two minutes.

Can’t I just use a free calculator or ChatGPT for this?

You can… and you’ll get a number. Payoff gives you a plan you can actually follow.

It starts with the money you already pay: it keeps your monthly total the same and rolls each finished card’s payment onto the next one, so you see how much sooner you’re out before adding a single extra dollar. Then it keeps everything in one place: your debts, five plans, your debt-free date, and exactly what to pay on each card, month by month. Change any number and it all updates on the spot.

Fair question, and we’d ask it too.

Payoff is software: we built it once, so we can sell it to a lot of people for very little. It’s a one-time payment, there’s no subscription, and it’s backed by our 30-day money-back guarantee (see our terms). We’d rather it be an insane value for you, so you tell a friend.

Is this a spreadsheet? Do I need Excel?

Nope! Payoff is an app that opens right in your phone’s browser. Nothing to download, no Excel, no Google Sheets, no formulas to break.

It works on your phone, tablet or computer.

How hard is it to use?

You enter 3 numbers for each debt: the balance, the interest rate and the minimum payment. They’re all on your monthly statement. Payoff does all of the math the moment you add them.

If you can type a number into a box, you can use this.

I have a LOT of debt. Will this still work?

Yes. Payoff handles up to 50 debts at once: credit cards, plus loans like car, student or personal loans.

The more debts you have, the more the order matters… and working out that order is exactly what Payoff does for you.

Does it connect to my bank? Is my information safe?

Payoff never asks for a bank login. You type in your balances yourself, and they’re saved on your own phone, not on our servers. We never see your numbers.
