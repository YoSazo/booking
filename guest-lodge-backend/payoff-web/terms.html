Marketel Payoff terms

Updated October 19, 2026

What you are buying

Marketel Payoff is a debt payoff planner that runs in your web browser. The regular price was $375 USD. The current sale price is $33 USD once. The optional cash-flow calendar costs $22 USD once, and the optional spending reset costs $9 USD once; both open inside Payoff. Extras start unselected. No subscription or automatic renewal is created.

Payment and delivery

You are charged at checkout. Payoff opens as soon as your payment is confirmed, and we email a link to the address supplied at checkout so you can open it again on any device.

Earlier paid preorders open Payoff, and any extras they included, with the link in your email.

30-day money-back guarantee

If Payoff is not for you, email support@bookmarketel.com with your order reference within 30 days of purchase for a full refund of your order, including any optional extras purchased with it. The 30 days begin on your payment date. Your applicable consumer rights are unaffected.

Planning estimates

Projections are educational estimates, not financial advice, lender statements or guaranteed outcomes. Interest is modeled as the yearly rate divided by 12 each month, with no fees, new borrowing or rate changes.

Your information

Stripe handles payment details. We use your checkout email to confirm your order and send your link. The balances, rates and payments you enter stay in your browser and are not sent to us. Anonymous page and checkout steps are recorded for product analytics.

Privacy policy · Contact Marketel

A checkout offer may save $5 on the Payoff plan. Open secure checkout within the displayed 30-minute window to claim it. Opening Stripe in time locks in the discounted price for that checkout. The offer applies to the base plan only; optional extras keep their listed prices.
