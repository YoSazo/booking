What you are buying

Marketel Payoff is a debt payoff planner that runs in your web browser. The regular price was $375 USD. The current sale price is $33.11 USD once. Buying Payoff creates no subscription or automatic renewal. Earlier orders that included the optional cash-flow calendar ($22 USD once) or spending reset ($9 USD once) keep them inside Payoff.

Your order includes a free $5 gift, applied to the base plan at checkout.

Payoff Coach (optional subscription)

The Payoff Coach is an optional subscription inside Payoff: answers to your questions about your own plan, lower-rate tools (call scripts, a comparison of a 0% balance transfer or consolidation loan against your current plan, and a promo-end tracker), the cash-flow calendar and the spending reset. It costs $9 USD a month or $67 USD a year, charged when you start it and then at the start of each month or year until you cancel. Three questions to the Coach are free with every Payoff order.

You can cancel any time in Payoff’s Coach tab (“Manage or cancel the Coach”) or by emailing support@bookmarketel.com. When you cancel, the Coach stays on until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again. Your first Coach payment is covered by the same 30-day money-back guarantee and Refund Eligibility Requirements as your Payoff order; later renewals are not refunded except where the law requires.

So the Coach can be started with one tap right after your purchase, Stripe keeps the card you paid with for that same visit; if you start the Coach, Stripe keeps that card on file to charge each renewal. We never see or store your card number. Apple Pay, Google Pay and other methods go through Stripe’s checkout.

The Coach gives education, not financial, legal or tax advice, and it can make mistakes. The dates and amounts in its answers are worked out by Payoff from the numbers you enter. The call scripts are suggestions for your own calls; card companies decide whether to lower a rate, refund a fee or offer a hardship program, and nothing in the Coach guarantees they will. Comparisons are estimates, before any credit check.
