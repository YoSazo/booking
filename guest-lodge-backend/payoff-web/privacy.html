Marketel Payoff Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect two types of information: personal data and non-personal data.

Personal Data: This includes information that can be used to identify you, such as your name, email address and billing information. We collect personal data when you purchase a product or contact us for support. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number. Stripe keeps the card you paid with for that same visit only, so an optional upgrade can be added with one click.

Non-Personal Data: This includes information that cannot be used to identify you, such as your browser type, referring site, and the date and time of each visit. We collect it to understand how visitors use our website and improve our services. The debts you enter in Payoff stay on your device and are not sent to us.

We may use your personal data to:

  • Process your order and send your access link.
  • Measure our advertising: your email address and name are hashed before they are sent to Meta.
  • Track your use of our website and services to improve them.
  • Disclose to law enforcement authorities or other third parties as required by law or to protect our rights.

2. Your Choices

You can choose not to provide certain personal data, but this may limit your ability to use certain features of our website and services.

You can request to access, update, or delete your personal data by contacting us at support@bookmarketel.com.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no website or internet transmission is completely secure.

4. Transfer of Data

Your information, including personal data, may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. These countries may have different data protection laws than your own country.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to track user activity on our website and remember preferences, including your free $5 gift. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect your use of our website.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@bookmarketel.com.

8. Refund Policy

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. To request a refund, please contact us at support@bookmarketel.com. Refund eligibility requirements are set out in our Terms & Conditions.
