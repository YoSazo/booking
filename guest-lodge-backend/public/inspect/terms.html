Inspect terms & privacy

Last updated September 19, 2026
Plain-language summary. Inspect organizes the photos and observations you provide. It does not inspect a property for you, certify its condition, determine responsibility, or replace a qualified professional. Finalized reports cannot be edited.

Reports and acceptable use

You are responsible for having permission to enter a property, photograph it, and share a report. Do not upload unlawful material or sensitive information that is unnecessary for a condition report. You are responsible for checking every observation and every optional AI wording suggestion before finalizing.

Marketel does not infer damage, cause, liability, repair cost, code compliance, habitability, safety, or authenticity. A timestamp records when our service received an upload; it does not prove when a photo was taken or that a report is complete.

Free building, single reports and plans

Building and previewing a report is always free. Inspect and Incident include one lifetime finalized report at no charge and without a card. Claims reports are free to build, and sending, sharing or downloading a finished Claims report requires either a single-report purchase of $12 USD or a plan. A single-report purchase is charged once, does not renew, and finalizes one report.

The paid plans are $25 USD per month or $199 USD per year. Both include unlimited finalized reports across Marketel's tools for one operator's own properties, subject to fair use of 300 finalized reports per monthly billing period (3,600 per yearly period). If you reach that, email support@bookmarketel.com and we will review it. A report can contain up to 100 photos and ten successful optional AI wording suggestions.

The first plan you start from a demo on our website begins with a 3-day free trial. You are not charged during it. The plan starts when the trial ends or when you send your first report, whichever comes first. Cancel before then, from your account screen, and you pay nothing. We email you the day before it starts.

Paid service begins only after you choose a single report or a plan and complete Stripe Checkout. Stripe stores and processes payment information. The subscription renews at the chosen monthly or yearly interval until canceled. You can manage or cancel renewal from your account screen. Canceling stops future renewal and does not delete existing reports.

Finalization, sharing and deletion

Finalizing consumes the applicable free report, plan allowance or single-report purchase and freezes that version. A finalized report shows the business name and logo on your account at that moment. Deleting a report does not restore allowance. Inspect and Claims reports can have a private share link that anyone holding it can open; Incident records cannot be shared by link. You may replace or revoke a report link, but copies already downloaded are outside Marketel's control. Claims produces documentation; it does not file, submit or manage claims with any platform or insurer.

Deleting your Inspect account permanently deletes its reports and queues stored photos for deletion, and cancels its separate Inspect subscription. It does not delete a Marketel booking property or booking subscription.

Data we process

We process your email (including one you enter to start building, before you verify it), authentication sessions, report text, property or unit labels, your business name and logo, uploaded photos, report activity and Inspect billing identifiers. Display-sized copies and original upload bytes are kept in private object storage. Originals are retained for report integrity and are not exposed through share links. We use this data to provide, secure and support Inspect and to meet legal obligations.

When you arrive from an advertisement, Inspect may store first-party advertising identifiers, campaign parameters, the landing URL, IP address and browser information. We use them to measure whether an advertisement led to a started report, finalized report, checkout or payment. Conversion events are sent server-to-server to Meta; Inspect does not load the Meta Pixel or give Meta access to report text or photos.

Optional AI wording help sends only the selected observation to the configured AI provider. The instruction is to preserve your facts and add none; you must review the result. Your original note remains unchanged unless you accept the suggestion.

Local drafts and photo files may remain on the device until you save, replace, sign out, or delete them. Email sign-in sessions expire. After account deletion, we retain a keyed, non-readable derivative of the email solely to enforce the one-lifetime-report limit. Operational records needed for security, billing and legal compliance may also be retained where required.

Availability and liability

Keep your own copy of important reports. The service is provided without a promise that it will be uninterrupted or appropriate for a legal, insurance, safety, move-in, move-out, or regulatory purpose. To the extent allowed by law, Marketel is not responsible for decisions made from an incomplete or inaccurate report.
