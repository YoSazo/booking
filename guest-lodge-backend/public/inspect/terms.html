Inspect terms & privacy

Last updated September 16, 2026
Plain-language summary. Inspect organizes the photos and observations you provide. It does not inspect a property for you, certify its condition, determine responsibility, or replace a qualified professional. Finalized reports cannot be edited.

Reports and acceptable use

You are responsible for having permission to enter a property, photograph it, and share a report. Do not upload unlawful material or sensitive information that is unnecessary for a condition report. You are responsible for checking every observation and every optional AI wording suggestion before finalizing.

Marketel does not infer damage, cause, liability, repair cost, code compliance, habitability, safety, or authenticity. A timestamp records when our service received an upload; it does not prove when a photo was taken or that a report is complete.

Free report and subscription

Each Inspect account includes one lifetime finalized report at no charge and without a card. The paid plan is $29 USD per month for one operator and up to 30 finalized reports in each billing period. A report can contain up to 100 photos and ten successful optional AI wording suggestions. Unused report allowance does not roll over.

Paid service begins only after you choose the plan and complete Stripe Checkout. Stripe stores and processes payment information. The subscription renews monthly until canceled. You can manage or cancel renewal from the Inspect account screen. Canceling stops future renewal and does not delete existing reports.

Finalization, sharing and deletion

Finalizing consumes the applicable free or paid report allowance and freezes that version. Deleting a report does not restore allowance. A private share link can be opened by anyone who has it. You may replace or revoke the link, but copies already downloaded are outside Marketel's control.

Deleting your Inspect account permanently deletes its reports and queues stored photos for deletion, and cancels its separate Inspect subscription. It does not delete a Marketel booking property or booking subscription.

Data we process

We process your email, authentication sessions, report text, property or unit labels, uploaded photos, report activity and Inspect billing identifiers. Display-sized copies and original upload bytes are kept in private object storage. Originals are retained for report integrity and are not exposed through share links. We use this data to provide, secure and support Inspect and to meet legal obligations.

When you arrive from an advertisement, Inspect may store first-party advertising identifiers, campaign parameters, the landing URL, IP address and browser information. We use them to measure whether an advertisement led to a started report, finalized report, checkout or payment. Conversion events are sent server-to-server to Meta; Inspect does not load the Meta Pixel or give Meta access to report text or photos.

Optional AI wording help sends only the selected observation to the configured AI provider. The instruction is to preserve your facts and add none; you must review the result. Your original note remains unchanged unless you accept the suggestion.

Local drafts and photo files may remain on the device until you save, replace, sign out, or delete them. Email sign-in sessions expire. After account deletion, we retain a keyed, non-readable derivative of the email solely to enforce the one-lifetime-report limit. Operational records needed for security, billing and legal compliance may also be retained where required.

Availability and liability

Keep your own copy of important reports. The service is provided without a promise that it will be uninterrupted or appropriate for a legal, insurance, safety, move-in, move-out, or regulatory purpose. To the extent allowed by law, Marketel is not responsible for decisions made from an incomplete or inaccurate report.
