Document guest damage while it is in front of you, and send a dated report before the claim window closes.

SNAP IT. SAY IT.
Take a photo of the damage and describe what you see out loud. Marketel writes the note for you. No typing, no forms.

EVERY PHOTO DATED
Each photo shows when it was taken and when Marketel received it. The unedited originals are kept as received, because some platforms ask for them.

KNOW YOUR DEADLINE
Enter the checkout date and Marketel tells you the date to file by — many platforms give hosts only 14 days after checkout.

A CLEAN REPORT IN MINUTES
Findings, dated photos, receipts and quotes, and your signature — signed once, reused with one tap. Send it as a private link or download the PDF.

BEFORE AND AFTER
Check a unit in at turnover with a few photos. Any damage report for that property shows the check-in as the "before", right beside the damage.

BUILT FOR HOSTS
Works for any short-term or vacation rental, whether you run one unit or many. Free to build a report; a Marketel plan is needed to send it.

Marketel reports record what the host observed and photographed. They are not valuations, cause determinations, insurance assessments or legal advice, and a report does not guarantee a claim is approved.
