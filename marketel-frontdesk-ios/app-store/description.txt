Run direct bookings and document property walkthroughs from one clear Marketel app.

MARKETEL INSPECT
Walk through a unit or property, group photos by room, record exactly what you observe, flag an issue, and produce a clean condition-report PDF before you leave. Finalized reports stay frozen, and private sharing links can be replaced or revoked.

Your first complete Inspect report is free with no card. Optional wording help can make your own observation clearer for review; it does not diagnose damage or add facts.

FRONT DESK FOR LODGING OPERATORS
Active Marketel booking customers can manage the direct booking page their guests use and the reservations that arrive through it.

BOOKINGS AT A GLANCE
See new and upcoming stays, review guest details, track booking status, and take action when a reservation needs attention.

AVAILABILITY THAT STAYS CLOSE
If a walk-in or another channel takes a room, record it in Availability or tell Front Desk Assistant. Marketel reduces the remaining availability shown on your direct booking page.

YOUR DIRECT BOOKING PAGE
Update rooms, rates, property information, and photos from the same system that receives your bookings.

NATIVE BOOKING ALERTS
Receive timely iPhone notifications for booking activity. Supported alerts let you confirm, release, or review a booking without searching through the app.

BUILT FOR SMALL OPERATORS
Inspect works for a property manager documenting units. Front Desk works for independent lodging teams running Marketel direct bookings. A simple product switch keeps each job focused.

Inspect reports record user-provided observations and are not professional building inspections, safety assessments, or legal certifications. Some Marketel booking features require an active property account.
