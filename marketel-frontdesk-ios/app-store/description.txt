Run your property’s direct bookings from one clear Front Desk.

Marketel Front Desk gives active Marketel customers a fast place to manage the booking page their guests use and the reservations that arrive through it.

BOOKINGS AT A GLANCE
See new and upcoming stays, review guest details, track booking status, and take action when a reservation needs attention.

AVAILABILITY THAT STAYS CLOSE
Open or close room nights, record walk-ins, and keep the availability shown on your direct booking page aligned with what is happening at the property.

YOUR DIRECT BOOKING PAGE
Update rooms, rates, property information, and photos from the same system that receives your bookings.

NATIVE BOOKING ALERTS
Receive timely iPhone notifications for booking activity. Supported alerts let you confirm, release, or review a booking without searching through the app.

BUILT FOR SMALL PROPERTIES
The interface stays focused on the daily decisions an independent property actually makes, with quick property switching for owners who manage more than one location.

Marketel Front Desk requires an active Marketel property account.
