Terms & privacy

In plain words. You pay $14.99 once and can trace as many pictures as you like, for good. There's no subscription and nothing renews. Your photos never leave your phone; what you type into "Describe it" is sent to OpenAI to draw it.

What you're buying

Marketel Trace shows a picture over your phone's camera so you can follow its lines on paper. Your first drawing is free. A one-time payment of $14.99 (US dollars, plus any tax shown at checkout) unlocks it on the phone you pay from, with a link we email you to unlock it on your other phones. It works in a web browser; there is nothing to install.

Refunds

If it doesn't work on your phone, email support@bookmarketel.com within 7 days of paying and we'll refund you in full.

Your photos and camera

The camera picture and any photo you choose are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't record or keep them.

Pictures you describe

When you use "Describe it", the words you type are sent to OpenAI to draw the picture, and the picture comes back to your phone. Don't type personal information. Your first AI picture is free; after you pay, 30 AI pictures a month are included. We may pause the drawing maker for the day when it's busy.

What we do record

We record anonymous steps, such as "opened the camera" or "opened checkout", with a random id kept in your browser, to see which parts of the page work. If you arrived from a Facebook or Instagram ad, we share that you started tracing, opened checkout or paid with Meta, together with the ad identifiers in your browser, so we can measure our ads. We use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use the page; it does not see your camera or photos. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number. Stripe gives us your email so we can send your receipt and unlock link.
